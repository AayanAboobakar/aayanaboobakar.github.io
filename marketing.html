How It Works

A calm, repeatable money workflow.

1. OnboardLearn the app flow on first launch, then start with a clean slate.
2. TrackAdd expenses quickly from transactions with amount, category, date, and note.
3. OrganizeShape your own category system with custom colors and icons.
4. BudgetSet monthly limits per category and monitor progress all month long.
5. ImproveUse analytics trends and comparisons to improve spending habits.

Why Fisca

Designed for clarity, not accounting complexity.

  • Manual-first by design: you log only what matters and stay intentional about each expense.
  • Private and lightweight: no forced account setup and no dependency on bank integrations.
  • Visual and readable: charts, progress indicators, and clear cards make finance less intimidating.
  • Flexible structure: custom categories plus fallback handling keep your data safe and organized.

FAQ

Quick answers

  • Does Fisca sync with banks? No. It focuses on manual expense tracking for control and simplicity.
  • Can I edit old entries? Yes. Transactions can be edited or deleted at any time.
  • Can I change currency and theme? Yes. Choose supported currencies and appearance mode in Settings.
  • Can I reset everything? Yes. You can reset stored data whenever you want a fresh start.
