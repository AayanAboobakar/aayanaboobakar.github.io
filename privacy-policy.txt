Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using our applications.

We do not require users to create an account and we do not directly collect personally identifiable information.

We do not transmit this data to our servers unless explicitly stated.

Our apps may use third-party services (such as advertising or analytics providers) to support functionality.

These services may collect limited information, which can include:

Device information
App usage data
Approximate location (e.g., via IP address)

This data is typically used for:

Delivering advertisements (personalized or non-personalized)
Improving app performance and user experience

Please refer to the respective third-party privacy policies for more details.

3. How Information Is Used

We use data only to:

Provide and maintain app functionality
Improve performance and user experience
Deliver advertisements where applicable

We do not sell or share user data with third parties beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

4. Data Storage and Control

User data is generally stored locally on the device unless otherwise specified.

Users have control over their data and may:

Modify or delete data within the app
Reset app data where such functionality is available
5. Children’s Privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user data. Where possible, data is stored locally on the device to minimize exposure.

We rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as analytics and advertising.
